Help

1. Logging in

Use the staff username and password given to you by the clinic administrator on the login page.

2. Registering a new appointment

  1. From the dashboard, click Register Appointment.
  2. Enter the patient's name, address and contact number, then choose the dentist and treatment from the dropdown lists.
  3. Pick a date (today or later) and a time.
  4. Click Register Appointment. If any field is invalid, the page will reload showing what needs fixing.
  5. Once saved, you'll see the appointment details and a generated appointment number (e.g. APT-000123) - make a note of it.

3. Searching for an appointment

  1. Click Search Appointment from the dashboard.
  2. Enter the appointment number and click Search.

4. Generating and printing a bill

  1. Search for the appointment.
  2. Click View / Generate Bill - the bill is created automatically the first time you open it, using the treatment's pricing rules.
  3. Click Print Receipt to print it.

5. Reports

From the dashboard, Today's Appointments lists everything scheduled for today, and Revenue by Treatment totals billed revenue per treatment.

6. Logging out

Click Logout in the top navigation bar.
